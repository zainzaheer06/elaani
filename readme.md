Primary User Types:
1. Platform Admin/Advertiser (What you currently have)

Manages the entire platform
Oversees all vendors and customers
Handles operations, analytics, marketing
Controls system settings and policies

2. Vendors/Partners (What you want to create)

Individual display equipment owners
Rent out their screens through the platform
Manage their inventory and earnings
Handle customer interactions

3. Customers/Renters

End users who rent displays
Browse available equipment
Make bookings and payments
Leave reviews and feedback

Secondary User Types:
4. Field Operations Staff

Technicians (installation/setup)
Delivery drivers
Maintenance personnel
Warehouse staff

5. Customer Service Representatives

Handle support tickets
Assist with bookings
Resolve disputes
Manage customer communications

6. Financial/Accounting Staff

Process payments and payouts
Handle invoicing and billing
Manage financial reports
Tax and compliance management

Specialized User Types:
7. Marketing Team
