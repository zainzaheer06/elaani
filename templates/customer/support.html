FAQ

How do I book a display?

You can browse available displays on the map or use our event planner to find the perfect fit. Click "Book Now" and follow the checkout process.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards (Visa, MasterCard), bank transfers, and other local payment methods. All payments are secured with SSL encryption.

Can I modify my booking?

Yes! You can modify or cancel bookings up to 48 hours before the event. Contact support for assistance.

Do you provide setup and installation?

Yes! Professional setup and installation are available for an additional fee. Select this option during booking.
